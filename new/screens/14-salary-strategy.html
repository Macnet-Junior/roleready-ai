🗣️ Negotiation Scripts

💬 Initial Counter

When they offer below your target

"Thank you for the offer. I'm excited about this opportunity. Based on my research and the market data for this role in San Francisco, I was expecting something closer to $220K base. Is there flexibility on the base salary?"

💬 Emphasizing Value

When you need to justify your ask

"In my current role, I led a product initiative that increased self-serve revenue by 40%. I believe that experience directly applies to the challenges you've described, and I'd like to discuss compensation that reflects that value."

When they won't budge on base

"I understand the base is fixed. Could we explore other areas? I'd like to discuss the stock grant, sign-on bonus, or a performance review at 6 months with a potential adjustment."

💬 Accepting

When the offer meets your target

"I'm pleased to accept this offer. Thank you for working with me on the compensation. I'm excited to start and contribute to the team."
