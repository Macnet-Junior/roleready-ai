# RoleReady — Legal Pages

## Privacy Policy

### What We Collect

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Email, name | Account creation | Until deletion |
| Resume content | AI analysis | Until deletion |
| Usage analytics | Product improvement | Anonymized after 30 days |
| Payment info | Billing | Never stored (Stripe handles) |
| IP address | Security, fraud prevention | 90 days |

### How We Use Your Data

```
✅ Provide and improve our services
✅ Personalize your experience
✅ Send you job matches and updates (with consent)
✅ Prevent fraud and abuse
✅ Comply with legal obligations

❌ We NEVER sell your data
❌ We NEVER use resumes for AI training (unless you opt in)
❌ We NEVER share data with advertisers
```

### Your Rights (GDPR)

```
Right to Access    → GET /api/user/export
Right to Rectify   → PUT /api/user/profile
Right to Erase     → POST /api/user/delete (30-day purge)
Right to Portability → GET /api/user/export (JSON format)
Right to Object    → POST /api/user/consent
Right to Restrict  → Contact privacy@roleready.ai
```

### Data Sharing

```
We share data ONLY with:
- Supabase (database, auth) — data processor
- Stripe (payments) — payment processor
- OpenAI/Google (AI analysis) — data processor, no training
- Resend (emails) — email delivery
- Vercel (hosting) — infrastructure

All processors are GDPR-compliant under Data Processing Agreements.
```

### Law Enforcement

```
We will disclose data ONLY when:
1. Valid legal process received (subpoena, court order, warrant)
2. Minimum data required
3. User notified (unless legally prohibited)
4. Legal review completed

We do NOT voluntarily share data with law enforcement.
```
